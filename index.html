05 / Share and expand

The next collection

Send a family tree

Relatives can export GEDCOM files from genealogy websites. Save them privately, review for conflicts, and add only confirmed people and relationships. Never automatically overwrite existing records.

A command-line preview importer is provided in the repository. It generates a review report without changing the family database.

Provenance first

Tell us the story behind the name

Family anecdotes, portraits, letters, places, memories and corrections can accompany a person's record. Different accounts should be preserved with their sources, not silently combined.

Private correspondence, living people's addresses, phone numbers and sensitive personal information should never be placed into public site files.
